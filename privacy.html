Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

freemindsearch publisher is a private tool used only by the freemindsearch team to publish freemindsearch's own videos to freemindsearch's own social media accounts (TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, Threads and others). It is not offered to the public, and no one else can sign in to it.

What data it uses

  • When the team signs in with TikTok, the app receives the basic profile of the freemindsearch account (account ID and display name) and an access token that lets it upload videos to that account.
  • It uploads only videos made by freemindsearch, with their captions.

What it does not do

  • It does not collect, read or store data about other TikTok users or viewers.
  • It does not sell or share any data, and it does not use data for advertising.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored only on the team's own computer, in the operating system's encrypted keychain. They are never published and can be revoked at any time from the account's settings on each platform.
