Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

This policy covers freemindsearch Studio, the desktop app the freemindsearch team uses to publish freemindsearch's own videos to freemindsearch's own social media accounts (TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, Threads and others), and this website. The app is not offered to the public, and no one outside the freemindsearch team can sign in to it.

What data the app uses

  • When the team signs in with TikTok, the app receives the basic profile of the freemindsearch account (account ID, display name and avatar), the account's posting settings (who can see posts, whether comments, Duet and Stitch are allowed, the longest video it can post), and an access token that lets it post videos to that account.
  • With the video list permission, it reads the public counts of freemindsearch's own videos (views, likes, comments, shares).
  • It uploads only videos made by freemindsearch, with their captions and the settings the team chooses for each post.

YouTube

freemindsearch Studio uses the YouTube API Services to upload freemindsearch's own videos to the freemindsearch YouTube channel (title, description, tags, and the "altered or synthetic content" setting) and to read that channel's own view and audience statistics. It does not access anyone else's YouTube data. Google's use of data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page.

What it does not do

  • It does not collect, read or store data about other TikTok users or viewers.
  • It does not sell or share any data, and it does not use data for advertising.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored only on the team's own computer, in the operating system's encrypted keychain. They are never published and can be revoked at any time from each platform's account settings.

This website

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no advertising. It is hosted on GitHub Pages.
