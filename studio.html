The app

freemindsearch Studio

freemindsearch Studio is the desktop app (macOS) the freemindsearch team uses to review, approve and publish freemindsearch's own films to its own accounts on TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, Threads and other platforms.

What it does

  • Review. Each finished film is shown with its script, its fact-checked claims and their sources, and its legal review. Nothing is published until a person approves it.
  • Publish. The team signs in to each platform with that platform's official login. For each approved film, the team chooses where to publish it and posts it through each platform's official API.
  • TikTok. The app uses TikTok's Login Kit (desktop) and Content Posting API. Before each post it shows the TikTok account it will post to, and the team chooses who can see the video, whether comments, Duet and Stitch are allowed, and whether the video promotes a brand, then edits the caption and posts. Every post carries TikTok's AI-generated content label. After posting, the app shows the video's status and, with the video list permission, its view, like, comment and share counts.
  • Numbers. The app reads the counts of freemindsearch's own posts (views, likes, comments, shares) so the team can see which stories people find most interesting.

Who can use it

The Studio is used by the freemindsearch team for freemindsearch's own accounts. It does not access other people's accounts or data. See the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Service.

Made with AI: the presenter, voice and visuals are AI-generated. Every story is researched and fact-checked by a person, with its sources listed.
