The wall sit that lowered blood pressure in a month

A new trial found that three wall sits a week lowered systolic blood pressure by about nine and a half points in four weeks, and once a week kept part of the drop. Inside your thighs, the tensed muscles squeeze your arteries, then let blood rush back when you stand; researchers think this helps vessels relax over time.
