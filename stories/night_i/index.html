Why dental implants get infected

Up to one in five people with dental implants develop an infection that destroys the jawbone. A Rutgers study in lab cells, tissue samples and mice says titanium particles trap immune cells and stop them clearing bacteria. Drugs targeting the pathway are not yet tested in people.
