A 38-year-old spy satellite just shattered in orbit. Nobody knows why

USA 32, a US spy satellite launched in September 1988 to listen in on Soviet radar and radio signals, broke apart about 775 km above Earth on 13 September 2026, the US Space Force confirmed. Nobody has said why, and it happened in a crowded lane of orbit, near where two satellites collided in 2009.
