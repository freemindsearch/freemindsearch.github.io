Did Neanderthals use their tool glue as medicine?

Birch tar is common at Neanderthal sites and was long thought to be just an adhesive. In a lab study, researchers made tar in several ways and found that most samples slowed the growth of a wound-infection bacterium, though far less than a modern antibiotic. It is not proof that Neanderthals treated wounds with it.
