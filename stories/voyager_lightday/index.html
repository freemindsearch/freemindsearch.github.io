Voyager 1 is about to be one light-day from Earth

On 18 November 2026, Voyager 1 becomes the first human-made object one light-day from Earth: a signal at the speed of light takes 24 hours to reach it. It launched in 1977 with 69 KB of memory, talks to us on a 22-watt radio, and engineers repaired it in 2024 from 24 billion kilometres away.
