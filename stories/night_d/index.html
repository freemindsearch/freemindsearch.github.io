An asteroid crater hides under the North Sea

Silverpit, a crater about three kilometres wide buried under the North Sea, was argued over for twenty years. New seismic images, shocked crystals from an oil well and computer models say an asteroid made it 43 to 46 million years ago. The asteroid size and the tsunami are model estimates.
