This shrimp makes a bubble almost as hot as the Sun

The pistol shrimp snaps its oversized claw so fast that the water tears into a bubble. When the bubble collapses, it reaches at least five thousand kelvin, flashes with light and makes one of the loudest sounds in the ocean. Sounds that shouldn't exist, part three.
