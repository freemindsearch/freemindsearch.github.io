These tiny animals survived 10 days in open space

In 2007, about three thousand dried tardigrades spent ten days in open space on the FOTON-M3 mission. Most came back alive and some laid eggs. One species' DNA-shielding protein even protects human cells, but a gun and a Moon crash found the limits of the toughest animal on Earth.
