A Mars Volcano May Be Hiding a Hot Spring Like Earth's First Life

A European spacecraft just picked up a gas near Mars's largest volcano, a possible sign that magma is still warm underneath it. If that heat ever meets water, it could make the same kind of hot spring where life may have begun on Earth.
