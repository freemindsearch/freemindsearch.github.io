Starship reached orbit with an engine dead

SpaceX's Starship reached orbit for the first time on 28 September 2026, even though one of the ship's engines failed eight minutes after liftoff. It released 26 third-generation Starlink internet satellites, then came home early: a deorbit burn about two hours in and a splashdown in the Pacific about three hours after launch.
