A 115-Million-Year-Old Dinosaur Still Has Blood In Its Bones

Scientists found mineralized red blood cells and heme molecules inside a 115-million-year-old dinosaur bone from Brazil, chemical proof that blood molecules can survive far longer than anyone assumed. It's the clearest confirmation yet of ancient dinosaur blood chemistry.
