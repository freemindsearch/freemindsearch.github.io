A Black Hole Is Firing a Beam of Particles at Earth

Astronomers just found the first microblazar in our own galaxy: a small black hole twelve thousand light years away, firing a jet almost straight at Earth. Its twin jet, aimed the other way, may be forging some of the most energetic particles ever detected in the Milky Way. Good news: by the time that beam reaches us, it is far too thin and faint to do any harm.
