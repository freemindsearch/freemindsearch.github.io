This sound was heard 3,000 miles away | Krakatoa 1883

In 1883, the eruption of Krakatoa made the loudest sound in recorded history: heard on Rodrigues Island, about 3,000 miles (4,800 km) away. Its pressure wave was recorded by barometers around the world, circling the planet several times.
