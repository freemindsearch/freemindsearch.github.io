Tesla's Roadster flew with a spacecraft trick

Tesla's new Roadster launched off a ramp, flipped and landed using cold-gas thrusters, the same idea spacecraft use to turn in orbit. The demo ran without a driver, the claimed speeds are Tesla's own and untested by outsiders, and the rocket feature is not approved for public roads.
