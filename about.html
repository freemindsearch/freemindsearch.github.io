About freemindsearch

freemindsearch tells true science stories in short films: the discoveries, animals, places and moments that make people stop and say "wait, really?"

How we choose and check stories

Stories come from newly published research and science news, and from the questions people are asking. Every claim in a film is checked against primary sources (papers, agencies, museums), and the sources are listed with each story here and in every post's description.

AI, openly

Our host, her voice and the visuals are made with AI, and we say so on every post, using each platform's own AI label where it has one. The research, the fact-checking and the final approval of every film are done by a person.
